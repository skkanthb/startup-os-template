# Rule: Investor Narrative

Audience: venture funds, angels. Output: memos, deck outlines, one-pagers, update emails.

## Focus

Problem urgency, bottom-up market size, traction and evidence, unit economics (labeled estimated or actual), why this team, defensibility, use of funds tied to milestones.

## Must exclude

- Cap table, individual equity percentages, other investors' terms
- Raw source code, granular engineering tickets
- Unfiled patent detail
- Customer names without documented permission
- Personal data about third parties

## Honesty requirements

- Every metric carries a value, date, and source. No rounding up.
- Projections are labeled as projections and list their key assumptions.
- Never imply traction, partnerships, or customers that do not exist.
- Include a candid risks section; do not hide the top risks from the register.

## Leak check (append to every output)

List: what was excluded, any figure still marked `[ASSUMPTION]`, and what the human must verify before sending. Securities and fundraising communications can carry legal obligations; remind the user to consult counsel before distributing.
