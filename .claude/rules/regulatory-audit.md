# Rule: Regulatory and Audit

Audience: compliance officers, legal counsel, auditors.

## Focus

Risk register and mitigations, data privacy and security controls, decision records (the audit trail), policy ownership, evidence locations.

## Must exclude

- Aggressive growth projections and sales scripts
- Investor and fundraising material
- Anything not true. Do not describe controls that are not implemented.

## Requirements

- Distinguish implemented, in progress, and planned controls.
- Point to evidence with `[EXTERNAL: ...]` locations rather than pasting it.
- Use ADRs and dated documents as the audit trail; note any gaps in history honestly.
- Remind the user that this output is not legal or compliance advice and should be reviewed by qualified counsel.

## Leak check (append to every output)

List excluded items, any control claim lacking evidence, and open gaps.
