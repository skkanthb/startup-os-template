# Rule: Technical Briefing

Audience: external CTOs, technical advisors, advisory board members.

## Focus

System design at a high level, key tradeoffs and their ADRs, scalability and failure modes, tech stack rationale, build/buy/open-source boundary, IP boundary at a safe-to-publish level.

## Must exclude

- Pricing models, commercial strategy, margins
- Investor terms and fundraising status
- Unfiled patent detail or novel mechanisms not yet protected
- Credentials, internal endpoints, security-sensitive configuration
- Customer-identifying data

## Handling IP

If the advisor may need detail on a novel mechanism, stop and tell the user: confirm an NDA and the patent situation first. Do not paraphrase the mechanism "loosely"; paraphrase is still disclosure.

## Leak check (append to every output)

List what was excluded and flag any sentence that describes how something novel works, for the human to review.
