# Rule: Candidate and Contributor Scoping

Audience: potential co-founders, technical hires, early contributors. Output location: `09-external-briefings/candidates/`.

## Focus

Problem domain, functional scope of the work, technical challenges, team culture and working style, what success looks like in the first 90 days.

## Must exclude

- Repository access or links to internal documents
- Cap table, equity terms (discuss in person, with counsel)
- Unfiled patent claims and novel mechanism detail
- Financial models, runway, fundraising status
- Names and details of customers, investors, or other candidates

## Process

1. Confirm whether an NDA is in place. If not, restrict to material safe for the public.
2. Scope to one task or one problem area, not the whole company.
3. Write as a standalone document with no links into the repo.

## Leak check (append to every output)

List excluded categories and any detail the human should double-check. If the candidate would be doing work that could produce IP, remind the user that IP assignment must be signed before they start.
