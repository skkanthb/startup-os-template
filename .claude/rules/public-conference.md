# Rule: Public Presentation

Audience: conferences, meetups, blog posts, social posts. Output location: `09-external-briefings/public_talks/`.

## Focus

Industry trends, the problem and why it matters, high-level vision, lessons learned, non-secret architecture concepts, your point of view.

## Must exclude

- Proprietary mechanics and unannounced features
- Anything that could be an unfiled invention (public disclosure can bar patent rights in many jurisdictions)
- Confidential metrics, customer names, investor information
- Quotes from private conversations

## Required step before drafting

Ask: "Is there anything in this talk you may want to patent?" If yes or unsure, stop and recommend speaking with a patent attorney before drafting the technical portions.

## After delivery

Log the disclosure (date, venue, what was said) in `03-product-and-technology/ip_and_patent_strategy.md` under the public disclosure log.

## Leak check (append to every output)

List excluded items, every sentence that describes how something works, and every number, for the human to verify as public-safe.
