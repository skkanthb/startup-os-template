# Rule: Strategic Partner Briefing

Audience: API, integration, and ecosystem partners. Output location: `09-external-briefings/partners/`.

## Focus

Joint value proposition, integration points and capabilities, co-marketing opportunities, mutual customer benefit, proposed pilot.

## Must exclude

- Internal financials, margins, runway
- Proprietary algorithms and implementation detail
- Cap table and investor information
- Other partners' terms or confidential discussions
- Roadmap commitments not yet decided

## Requirements

- State what each side contributes and what each side gets.
- Mark anything not agreed as "proposed".
- Confirm an NDA before sharing anything beyond public material.

## Leak check (append to every output)

List excluded items and any statement that could be read as a commitment, for the human to confirm.
