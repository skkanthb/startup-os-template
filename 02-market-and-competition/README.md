# 02 Market and Competition

**Purpose:** external reality. Market size, competitors, and what customers actually said. This folder is where assumptions get tested against evidence.

**Sensitivity:** internal. Anonymize individuals in interview logs (role and company type, not names) in case parts are later shared.

## Files

| File | Answers |
|---|---|
| `market_research.md` | How big is the market (bottom-up first), and what tailwinds or headwinds apply? |
| `competitive_analysis.md` | Who else solves this, how do we differ, and how defensible are we? |
| `customer_pain_points.md` | What did real customers say and do? Which hypotheses are validated? |

## How to use

Start with `customer_pain_points.md` (talk to people), then size the market, then map competitors. Competitor facts go stale: record the date each was checked.

## For AI assistants

- Every number needs a source with a date, or an `[ASSUMPTION]` tag. Do not supply market-size figures from memory as if verified; if asked to research, cite sources and say when data may be dated.
- Competitor comparisons: mark unknown cells "unknown", never guess.
- Interview content is evidence. Quote it faithfully and do not generalize one conversation into a pattern.
- Root rules: `../CLAUDE.md` (or `../AGENTS.md`).
