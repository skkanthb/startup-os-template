# 06 Team and Organization

**Purpose:** who is on the team, who should be, and how people work together. Founder roles and rationale, hiring plan, and onboarding.

**Sensitivity:** high. Roles and rationale only. No equity percentages, vesting terms, salaries, or individual offers; reference signed documents as `[EXTERNAL: ...]`. Not legal advice.

## Files

| File | Answers |
|---|---|
| `cofounder_agreements.md` | Who decides what, what happens in disagreement, what is the rationale for the arrangement? |
| `hiring_roadmap.md` | Which capability gaps block the next milestone, and who fills them? |
| `onboarding_guide.md` | What should a new teammate or advisor with full access read and do first? |

## How to use

Complete the founder file before anyone else joins. Use the onboarding guide only for people granted full access; for everyone else generate a scoped briefing.

## Related

## For AI assistants

- Refuse to record equity splits, vesting schedules, or compensation figures. Tell the user to store them outside git.
- For outside candidates, use `../.claude/rules/candidate-scoping.md`, not the onboarding guide.
- Do not store private personal information about team members or candidates.
- Root rules: `../CLAUDE.md` (or `../AGENTS.md`).
