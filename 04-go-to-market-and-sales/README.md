# 04 Go-to-Market and Sales

**Purpose:** how you reach, convince, and keep customers. Positioning, pricing hypotheses, buyer profiles, and working sales material.

**Sensitivity:** internal. Pricing logic and margins must not appear in customer-facing outputs; use the enterprise-sales rule when generating anything for buyers.

## Files

| File | Answers |
|---|---|
| `gtm_strategy.md` | Which segment first, what positioning, pricing, channels, and acquisition loop? |
| `ICP_and_personas.md` | Who exactly is the customer and the buying committee? |
| `sales_enablement.md` | Discovery questions, objection handling, ROI framework, battlecards, pilot playbook |

## How to use

Define the ICP first, then positioning and channels, then enablement material. Treat pricing as a hypothesis until customers pay.
