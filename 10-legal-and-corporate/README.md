# 10 Legal and Corporate

**Purpose:** an index of where legal and corporate documents live and what state they are in. It is an index, not a vault.

**Sensitivity:** high. Do not store signed documents, contract terms, or counsel's advice here. Reference them as `[EXTERNAL: ...]`. Nothing in this repo is legal advice.

## Files

| File | Answers |
|---|---|
| `corporate_and_ip_index.md` | What is our entity, which documents exist and where, what compliance obligations and deadlines apply, who is our counsel? |

## How to use

Keep the document index current as agreements are signed. Review deadlines monthly.

## Related

IP strategy: `../03-product-and-technology/ip_and_patent_strategy.md`. Founder agreements rationale: `../06-team-and-organization/`.

## For AI assistants

- Do not draft binding legal terms as if final, and do not give legal conclusions. Draft for counsel review and say so.
- Never paste contract clauses, ID numbers, or account details into this folder.
- Flag missing basics (IP assignments, NDAs, entity good standing) when you notice them.
- Root rules: `../CLAUDE.md` (or `../AGENTS.md`).
