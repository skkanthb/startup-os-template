# 12 Retrospectives and Pivots

**Purpose:** institutional memory of what you tried, learned, and changed. This is the folder that explains why the company looks the way it does.

**Sensitivity:** internal. Honest write-ups are the point; failed bets are valuable data.

## Files

| File | Answers |
|---|---|
| `pivot_log.md` | What did we believe, what did we learn, what did we change, and what did we stop doing? |
| `retrospective_template.md` | Copy per period to review goals, what worked, what did not, and what to focus on next |

## How to use

Log a pivot entry whenever direction changes materially. Copy the retrospective template to `YYYY-MM_retro.md` at each period end, and update the assumptions log from what you learned.

## Related

Decisions: `../03-product-and-technology/adr/`. Assumptions: `../08-risks-and-mitigations/blindspots_and_assumptions.md`.

## For AI assistants

- Do not rewrite or soften past entries. Add new entries above the line in the log; the history is the value.
- When a pivot is logged, list the documents that now need updating and ask before editing them.
- Do not turn a failure into a success narrative. Record what happened.
- Root rules: `../CLAUDE.md` (or `../AGENTS.md`).
