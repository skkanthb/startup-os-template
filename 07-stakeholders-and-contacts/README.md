# 07 Stakeholders and Contacts

**Purpose:** a working map of advisors, investors, partners, prospects, and experts, plus a log of relationship changes and next steps.

**Sensitivity:** medium-high. Business-relevant context only. No private personal details. Contact details (emails, phone numbers) belong in your CRM or address book, not git.

## Files

| File | Answers |
|---|---|
| `stakeholder_map.md` | Who matters, how much influence and interest, what we need and what they want, who owns the relationship |
| `CRM_logs.md` | What happened in each interaction and what is the next step |

## How to use

Update the log after meaningful interactions (newest first). Review the stakeholder map's top-five priority list weekly.

## Related

Investor pipeline specifically: `../05-investors-and-fundraising/fundraising_tracker.md`.

## For AI assistants

- Do not add private personal information about individuals. Keep entries to what affects decisions and follow-ups.
- When asked what to tell a stakeholder, pick the matching audience rule in `../.claude/rules/` first.
- Surface overdue follow-ups when doing a weekly review.
- Root rules: `../CLAUDE.md` (or `../AGENTS.md`).
